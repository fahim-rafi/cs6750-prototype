Scope of the redesign

What changed from LinkedIn Jobs?

Only two existing task surfaces are redesigned: search results + job details and the Saved tab in Job Tracker. Navigation, search, browsing, and save/unsave interactions are intentionally familiar.

  1. Result preview: display remote status, employment type, salary availability, and missing information before opening a listing. A contract role is visible as a mismatch immediately.
  2. Task-relevant filters: make Remote in Canada and Permanent easily accessible without changing the rest of the search flow.
  3. Job-detail rationale: distinguish preference fit from skill/profile fit, and show specific constraints rather than an unexplained recommendation label.
  4. Shortlist comparison: select jobs while browsing or from Job Tracker; compare them side by side, write notes, and keep the existing reversible Save behavior.

These are design hypotheses derived from survey, apprenticeship, and heuristic findings—not proven improvements. This is a standalone simulated prototype for evaluation.
